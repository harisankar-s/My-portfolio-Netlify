About me

I've spent over ten years turning ambiguous, high-stakes problems into data systems that work: distributed processing with Spark and Kafka, the modern data stack (dbt, Great Expectations, DataHub), and multi-cloud platforms on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, with Snowflake and Databricks. More recently I build production GenAI on top of that data, with RAG, agents and Claude.

I own delivery end to end: shaping the architecture, mentoring engineers, running quality audits and keeping stakeholders aligned.
